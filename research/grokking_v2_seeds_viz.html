What it means

The speedup is entirely a post-memorization effect. Since switching the filter on at memorization (~epoch 130) matches running it from the start, the filter does nothing to how the model memorizes — it accelerates the generalization transition that weight decay drives afterward. The filter doesn't create grokking (it can't, without weight decay), but it sharpens and speeds the cleanup once the norm pressure is pushing toward the efficient circuit.
