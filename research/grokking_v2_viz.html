Three findings

1. The filter accelerates grokking when weight decay is present

Filter+AdamW groks at epoch 2650 vs the baseline's 3700, with a sharper transition (~500-epoch ramp vs ~750). Single seed — the direction is safe, the 28% magnitude needs 3–5 seeds.

2. The filter does NOT substitute for weight decay

Filter+Adam with no weight decay never groks (final test = chance). Grokking needs the norm-reduction pressure of weight decay; the filter provides none.

3. The filter does NOT trigger generalization on demand

Turning the filter on right after memorization (the "switch" run) does nothing without weight decay.

Why: the filter rides the dominant direction — it doesn't choose the good one

MNIST label noise (mini-batch): memorizing corrupted labels = transient, per-batch directions; true signal recurs. The filter keeps the persistent (signal) directions. Filter helps because signal is the persistent direction.

Grokking (full-batch): no sampling noise. The memorizing solution is itself a globally persistent direction, so the filter projects right onto it. Generalization comes only from weight decay's separate norm pressure — the filter just accelerates a transition weight decay is already driving.

Bonus: grokking gradient dynamics are ultra-low-dimensional

Filter effective rank (entropy of the eigenvalue spectrum) sits at 2–8 during grokking — versus 60–85 on MNIST. The full-batch grokking gradient lives in a tiny subspace, consistent with the "small set of Fourier features" picture. The rank=200 cap never binds.
