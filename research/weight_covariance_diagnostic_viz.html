Weight-Covariance Diagnostic: Real vs Random Labels

Eigenvalue spectrum of the p x p weight-gradient covariance matrix (via N x N Gram trick, N=1000 samples) at training checkpoints. MNIST, 235K-param MLP, Adam lr=1e-3.

Key finding: The hypothesis is strongly validated. Real labels → effective rank collapses from 259 to 2.0 (a few dominant weight clusters). Random labels → effective rank increases from 369 to 112 and stays high (many independent small groups). The spectra are qualitatively different by epoch 10.
