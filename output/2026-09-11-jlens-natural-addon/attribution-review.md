## Method and provenance

The [screen source](screen_attribution.py) was committed at `8377b28` before
the sole run. Five fabricated parser/redirect checks passed before release;
then context-associated anchor preservation was added and reviewed before
that freeze. The script read only the frozen attribution manifest and 96
pre-enumerated URLs: article, first history, current talk for each selected row.
Serial unauthenticated GETs, descriptive contact User-Agent, no retries or
automatic redirects, 20-second request timeout, 2 MiB response bound. HTML
stayed in memory; compact contexts/links/status/byte counts/hashes were saved.
No linked references, source articles, older histories or talk archives fetched.

limit. The screen completed at 2026-09-11T05:27:35.562024Z in 61.195 seconds;
saved output totals 1,273,578 bytes. Receipt (artifact not distributed in this public snapshot)
SHA256 `c7b4fe67ef0543a217d29c897213e8a6ad2ab67db060f5ff02d99b766d0e6a7a`.
Main independently verified all 96 record hashes, URL/count/status bindings,

All article footers identify CC BY-SA 4.0. The raw field
`cc_by_sa_4_link_present` is false because it checks a direct Creative Commons
URL, whereas these pages link to Wikipedia's local copy of the license.
The preserved anchor text and target establish the footer observation; false
does **not** mean the footer or license was absent. Raw records remain unchanged.

This implements the frozen [protocol](protocol.md), informed by Wikimedia's
[reuse and additional-attribution terms](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use#7._Licensing_of_Content)
and the [CC BY-SA 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Current pages are not an atomic reconstruction of the collector's separately
reported revisions. Applicable source-author chains and the legal status of
every underlying contribution were not independently established.

No model, scientific scores, activations, reader choices or grading were
accessed by this screen. This changes attribution readiness, not utility evidence.
