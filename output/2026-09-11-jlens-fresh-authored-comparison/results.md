# New content: useful correspondence, no primary added advantage

Codex — Spectral Optimizer Investigation · J-Lens · 11 September 2026

The unchanged direction-token lists help readers predict local model-score
ordering on prospectively fixed new content. However, ordinary fit examples
work just as well on the primary direction. This is a useful local signal,
not evidence that J-Lens is superior to examples or that four semantic
clusters have been discovered.

Evidence: provisional, eight hypothesis-guided contents in two forms, one
frozen model/layer, four history-free same-parent AI readers. These are not
256 independent examples, human judgments or independent model families.

## What was actually tested

One isolated author supplied eight contrasts before measurement, excluding
203 declared previous action lemmas/spelling aliases. All valid entries were
retained unchanged, including recorded ambiguities. This establishes only
declared stimulus-inventory disjointness, not pretraining novelty. The author
knew the abstract observation/preparation hypothesis, so this is not a
representative natural-language sample.

Original model, PCA directions, signs, source mean and fit references were
unchanged. One32-sentence frozen-model pass captured only the final verb
subtoken; readers saw the full prefix through that verb. Four homogeneous
reference packets were judged once, and all256 first choices were committed
before score inspection. No repair, best-reader selection or new decoding.
Raw examples and direction-token lists are not information-matched; reader
and arm effects are mixed and each reader sees multiple axes.

The separate saved-array checker verifies128 scalars and64 gaps, with maximum
arithmetic discrepancy2.220446049250313e−16. Main's independent JSON audit
and the Astra saved-result review (artifact not distributed in this public snapshot)
agree on all256 graded judgments. Execution/source pins are in
execution.md (artifact not distributed in this public snapshot) and [source-review.md](source-review.md).
