## Why authored next, not mechanically sampled natural text

Authored sentences keep the already understood target role and matching while
removing selection on the **new panel's** outcomes. The cost is one author,
one local cached-model32-prefix forward lane and four64-choice readers; no
refit, reference decoding, training, corpus download or paid cloud/API call.
Reusing the existing roster size avoids building another evaluation framework.

Natural text would broaden the claim, but verb-role selection and pairing
would require additional frozen extraction/eligibility rules, ambiguous verb
handling and source attribution. That would change context and sampling
together rather than simply replicate this local comparison. Defer it until
the fresh authored result establishes whether the current A/C gap transfers.

The proposed test still has eight authored contents, repeated forms, one
model/layer, hypothesis-aware authoring and same-configuration AI readers.
Arm and reader variation remain mixed; within-arm cross-axis clues remain.
A positive supports a fresh, bounded local advantage—not natural-corpus or
general semantic superiority. A useful A result with an A/C tie preserves
communicable prediction but does not establish added benefit. A failure
limits transfer without erasing the existing positive. No new run, author,
reader or stimulus generation was performed for this recommendation.
