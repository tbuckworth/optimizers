# Spectral directions beside individual-example readouts

Codex — Spectral Optimizer Investigation · 10 September2026

**Complete qualitative illustration, not a new semantic score.** The
standalone reader (artifact not distributed in this public snapshot) shows all four signed activation covariance
directions beside individual J-Lens readouts of exactly the same held-out
endpoint prefixes. Every column retains all12 ranked token strings.

The useful distinction is visible without another model run: an individual's
readout often expands the prefix's own verb—such as “implemented” or “lifted”—
whereas PC3's positive readout emphasizes testing/evaluation. PC1's positive
list emphasizes imaging rather than just the particular act of measuring.
These are unblinded descriptions of the displayed strings, not quality ratings
or established abstractions. The other poles matter: PC2's negative list has
cooking words although the selected example is programming, and PC4's imaging
list accompanies the kitchen endpoint. All such mismatches remain displayed.

Thus there is something interpretable to inspect, but a direction's readable
words are not automatically a faithful label for every example with a large
loading. This is consistent with the earlier limited pole-matching result;
the new display adds no grades, human/AI judgments or numerical superiority
claim. It does not identify four distinct semantic clusters.

## What the comparison does and does not equalize

Each arm has two lists of12 ranked strings and the same two displayed prefixes.
The signed PC pair summarizes32 fit rows. The two individual vectors directly
encode those held-out examples. They therefore use different underlying
information despite equal display length. The individual vector on a negative
PC endpoint is raw h for that example, **not −h**. Fit-individual, centered-
individual and rank-four-reconstructed readouts were not retained.

The original input may contain a mean component, several PC components and a
residual; an individual PC is only one direction in that representation.
Consequently it need not reproduce the original input's readout. Token rankings
are not additive vector components. The useful prospective question is whether
these shared-direction descriptions explain new examples, not whether they
copy the same endpoint words or win a different attribution task.
