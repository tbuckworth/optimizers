## Overlap and interpretation limits

Main independently confirmed all 16 selected past-tense surface forms are
absent as whole words from the five authored rosters listed by Astra, with
all their hashes matching. Main additionally checked the completed 24-prefix
Wikipedia dataset, SHA
`7391a1fa6c02872cdeabb2e8dffc1e63181b6379d80e2042f33b38a61308ff02`,
and found no exact whole-word matches there either. Related lemmas, concepts,
entities, templates and decoder words can overlap. No lemma/subtoken or full
pretraining disjointness was tested. The extra metadata check initially used
the wrong parent path for Wikipedia; locating `excerpts-resumed/dataset.json`
resolved it without acquisition or modification of that dataset.

The causal verb prefixes omit different parts of the full proposition in the
two templates. Token identity, frequency, morphology, position and meaning
co-vary. Some O/P actions overlap in function; they are not logical opposites.
A local positive can be useful lexical/contextual correspondence without a
single abstract causal concept, unique PC4 semantics, general J-Lens advantage
or optimizer/safety implication. This design does not test all those claims.
