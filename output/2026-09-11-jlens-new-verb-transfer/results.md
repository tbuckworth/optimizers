## Outcome and implication

The unchanged observation/recording versus provision/preparation interpretation
predicts **16/16 positive PC4 verb-position differences** on the fixed new panel.
All eight contents pass in both active and passive form, with no zeros or
template sign changes. The predeclared strong all-positive prediction passes.
Sentence-final period also gives 16/16, descriptively rather than as an alternate
success criterion. This is useful transfer beyond the original selected verb
forms, not just a reanalysis of the original four contrasts.

The positive survives the ambiguities declared before measurement, including
copying/transcription also creating useful records and mailing dispatching
information. Those cases were not dropped, relabelled or replaced. Their
success does not settle the right verbal taxonomy; O and P are not exclusive
logical categories. It does show that the fixed signed forecast worked for
those preselected examples.

The right claim is **useful local predictive correspondence in this chosen
panel**, not four distinct clusters, a uniquely semantic PC4, a causal feature,
general J-Lens superiority or a safety/optimizer result. The other directions
also respond. Eight chosen contents expressed twice are not 16 independent
content samples. No confidence interval or p-value is assigned.
No new readers made judgments: 16/16 is the success count of a fixed signed
forecast, not a new blinded-reader matching accuracy.

## What this adds, and what it leaves unchanged

The [earlier local result](../2026-09-11-jlens-endpoint-location/results.md)
used four reused contrasts and found 8/8 positive at both local roles.
This test keeps the direction, sign and interpretation but changes to eight
other verb contrasts selected and committed before their model scores.
That is a substantive extension of useful correspondence. The author knew
the research history; this is not independent hypothesis invention or a
representative random sample of language. “New” is only the absence of exact
chosen verb forms from the checked previous inputs, not unseen pretraining
vocabulary, new lemmas or concepts.

The old later-ending [1/8 positive result](../2026-09-11-jlens-content-template/results.md)
remains unchanged. The new strings do not include that extra sentence, so
their later-ending behavior is unmeasured. The old external-text 19/24 and
weak all-axis comparator totals are different tasks and must not be pooled
with these signed forecasts. A useful contrast can be real without being
exclusive to one axis or better than example-based interpretation.

The coherent synthesis (artifact not distributed in this public snapshot)
gives the positive population-regression explanation for why a direction
readout can help. This new result supports predictive transfer of the current
gloss, not all assumptions of that mechanism or a causal Jacobian claim.
