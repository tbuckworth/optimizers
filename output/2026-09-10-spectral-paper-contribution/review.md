## Independent review: what is and is not missing

The leaf concluded that neither familiarity nor persistence is necessary for
the current claim. If another learning-history question is selected, it favors
equal-total-exposure early/late timing over persistence. Required interpretation:

- A selective native rescue with common protection would support moderation by
  learning history, not an identified latent familiarity mechanism.
- Similar improvement in both policies would establish a timing effect but
  little support for a spectral-specific familiarity account.
- No native rescue despite genuine early rare competence would make absent
  warmup exposure insufficient in that recipe, not prove universal rejection.
- Failure to establish early competence would make the manipulation inadequate
  for that explanation; do not interpret its endpoint as a decisive negative.

Main and leaf agree that moving full indexed (image, assigned-label) occurrences
is essential: regenerating labels across the clean/noisy switch changes the
corruption dose. Exposure before the filter switch also changes which optimizer
learns the rare examples. A useful result could be Adam acquisition followed
by spectral preservation. Model, Adam and observer histories change jointly.
