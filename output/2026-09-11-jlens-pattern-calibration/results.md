## The simple comparison

new Wikipedia prefixes forming16within-topic pairs, at the final actual
subtoken of each unchanged prefix. Both receive the same original positive/
negative examples and12vocabulary tokens per pole. Control U uses original
signed-u tokens. Treatment P uses signed normalized pattern tokens fitted on
64different prefixes/32pairs. No model training, PCA refit, score-sign change,
new exemplar or outcome-based axis selection occurred.

For calibration pair i, δh_i=h_L,i−h_R,i and δz_ij=δh_iᵀu_j. The new
description vector is d_j=(Σ_i δh_i δz_ij)/(Σ_i δz_ij²). It is an activation
regression pattern for the unchanged score, not another PCA vector or cluster.
All pair weights are one; all four denominators passed the fixed zero-only
rule. Normalization is applied before the same frozen J-Lens decoder. The
[protocol](protocol.md) and [calibration audit](calibration-audit.json) retain

## What changes in the interpretation

The proposed population correction is mathematically feasible and implemented
as specified, but this finite recipe has not established overall descriptive
utility. Its local PC4 positive preserves an affirmative line: a calibrated
display can improve this direction's score predictions while other displays
get worse. It does not identify why—finite-sample estimation, changed population
mixtures, normalized vocabulary compression and reader inference remain possible
contributors. This test does not separate them. More attractive words or the
exact uᵀd=1 identity would not answer that question either.

The prior [natural-text add-on](../2026-09-11-jlens-natural-addon/results.md)
gave74/128 versus75/128 with/without original tokens; today's panel and contrast
are different, so61/128 is not a measured decline from75/128. Earlier selected
and fresh local action-word positives remain valid in their scope. The
blunt whole-investigation verdict (artifact not distributed in this public snapshot)
does not change: conditional interpretive promise, not a robust general-purpose
tool. Our PCA-direction application is not a negative verdict on J-Lens generally.

Decision: complete reporting and consolidate this boundary into the coherent
research account. No new model, reader, corpus, alternate axis, ridge choice,
larger PCA fit or near-duplicate panel is selected by this result. Any later
test needs a distinct predictive question, not another chance to obtain a win.
