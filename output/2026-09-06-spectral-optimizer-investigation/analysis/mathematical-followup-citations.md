# Citation registry: mathematical follow-up primary literature

- `ghashami2016frequent`: Deterministic streaming matrix sketch with spectral/projection approximation guarantees for its own update.
- `gurari2018tiny`: Observes a small, persistent Hessian-related gradient subspace in studied deep-learning settings.
- `kunstner2019empirical`: Shows that empirical Fisher is generally not a reliable curvature/natural-gradient surrogate.
- `zhang2017rethinking`: Demonstrates that standard deep networks can fit random labels.
- `arpit2017memorization`: Reports empirical separation of simple-pattern learning and later noise memorization.
- `wilson2017marginal`: Shows adaptive methods can select qualitatively different solutions from GD/SGD.
- `zhao2024galore`: Per-gradient-matrix low-rank projection for optimizer-state memory reduction.
- `jacot2018ntk`: Establishes the frozen/infinite-width neural-tangent-kernel training formulation.
- `rahaman2019spectral`: Fourier/function-space spectral bias, distinct from parameter-gradient covariance spectra.
